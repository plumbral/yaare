Yaare built-in terminal palettes

These are readability adaptations, not unmodified upstream themes.
Yaare adjusts low-contrast foregrounds and selected text to 4.5:1.
ANSI black is preserved for terminal application backgrounds; xterm minimum
contrast correction protects foreground text, including ANSI/256/true color.
The settings preview displays the corrected black foreground.

Only palette data and notices are bundled. No upstream plugins, images,
editors or extra fonts are included. All palettes work offline.
